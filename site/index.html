How to play

Chips

Tables only take chips. At the cashier, buy chips with cash ($1, $5, $10, $50, $100, $1K, $5K) or cash them back in. Leaving cashes out everything at face value.

Blackjack

Beat the dealer to 21 without going over. Blackjack pays 3 to 2. The dealer stands on all 17s. You can double on your first two cards and split a pair once.

Roulette

Pick a chip, then tap the board (on a small screen, swipe it sideways to see every number). Aim at a number's edge for a split, or a corner for four numbers, and press and slide to adjust before you lift. Single zero. A straight-up number pays 35 to 1.

Slots

Set a bet and spin. Three matching symbols pay big. Tap Pays to see the table.
